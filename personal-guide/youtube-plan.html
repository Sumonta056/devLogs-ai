AI & Future of Software Engineering
@thebat4242AI Fear👍 3
Vaiya is doing Software engineering course in 2026 still worth it?? Will AI tools become a danger for new software engineers? Whats your pov?
@codecareergolpo5638 replied:
Software engineering will always be worth it but survival will be tougher. AI is currently a danger only for junior engineers because clients prefer to give their AI tools to seniors. But in 3–4 years, companies will realise they need juniors again — there won't be future seniors if no juniors are trained now.
@asikulislamsiyam8601AI + CSE Future👍 2
Bro everyone said that cse era is end. There will be no vacancy for cse job in market... what do you think about this? I have learned nothing in 1.5 years except basic code/DSA to pass the exam...
@codecareergolpo5638 replied:
The Computer Science era isn't ending, but we're in a slowdown. Entry-level roles are shifting. Most clients are hesitant to hire juniors right now — but this is temporary. Companies still hiring! Focus on getting better, not on market panic.

I use AI but only sometimes for code generation. Coding is only 10% of the job — the other 90% is communicating with stakeholders, planning, investigating. For coding specifically, I'd say 40–50% AI-assisted. But my main job is end-to-end task ownership, not just writing code.

University & Education
@md.atikullah6076University Choice👍 3
Vai sust software engineer naki brac CSE which is best for my career?

SUST gives mandatory internship, great networking with seniors at good companies, but has session jam and fewer teachers. BRAC CSE is top-ranked private university. Both are among Bangladesh's best. The only real difference is the financial requirement for private university.

Tech Stack & Skill Questions
@atking7678Stack Choice
I'm confused. I know React, Next.js, Node, Express, NestJS, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, Mongoose, Prisma. For companies like KAZ, Cefalo, Enosis, Brain Station 23... Should I go: (1) .NET + DSA or (2) deepen MERN + Python + AI/LLM engineering?
@codecareergolpo5638 replied:
Don't be an all-rounder. You cannot master .NET + MERN + Python + LLM. That is the most destructive thing you can do to yourself. Pick ONE path: MERN, .NET, or Python/AI. Go all-in on it. You already know MERN — try to get a job first with that, get industry experience, then consider switching stacks.

My current stack: Java + Spring Boot (backend), Vue + Nuxt (frontend). As a beginner, master the basics and fundamentals. The stack doesn't matter much — as an SE you will work on different stacks based on project demands. I will make a guideline video soon!
@MahediHasanEmon-f3fCP / CF Rating

They don't judge you directly on CF rating. You can have a lower rating but be a very good problem solver — that's what matters most. There is no specific range; the target is problem-solving ability, not the rating itself.
