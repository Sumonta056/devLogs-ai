🏦 Bank Deposits — FDR & DPS

সবচেয়ে নিরাপদ বিনিয়োগ — ফিক্সড ডিপোজিট ও মাসিক সঞ্চয়

💰 FDR (Fixed Deposit Receipt) — Explained

You deposit a lump sum amount in a bank for a fixed period (3 months to 5 years). The bank pays you interest. At maturity, you get your money + profit back.

✅
Key point: In 2026, Bangladesh Bank's tight monetary policy has pushed FDR rates to decade-highs. Private banks are offering 9.5%–11.5% for 1-year tenures. This is a great time for FDR!

⚠️
Tax note: Banks deduct 10% source tax on FDR interest if you have a TIN. Without TIN, they deduct 15%. So ৳1,000 interest becomes ৳900 or ৳850 in hand.

📅 DPS (Deposit Pension Scheme) — Monthly Savings

You save a fixed amount every month for a set period (usually 3–10 years). At maturity, you get the total savings + compounded interest. Think of it as a forced savings habit.

FeatureDetails
Minimum monthly৳500/month (most banks)
Typical tenure3 years, 5 years, 7 years, or 10 years
Interest rate8%–10% (varies by bank & tenure)
Missing a paymentUsually a ৳50–100 fine. Too many misses = account closed
Early withdrawalPossible but with penalty — you get less interest
Loan against DPSMost banks allow up to 80–90% loan against your DPS
💡
Example: Save ৳2,000/month in DPS at 9% for 5 years → You deposit ৳1,20,000 total → You get back approximately ৳1,55,000–1,60,000 at maturity. That's ~৳35,000–40,000 profit just from discipline!

📝 How to Open FDR/DPS

1

Choose a bank

Compare rates above. Prioritize banks where you already have a savings account.

2

Visit the branch or app

Many banks now allow online FDR/DPS opening through their app (BRAC Bank, City Bank, DBBL). Bring NID + photo if visiting.

3

Fill the application form

Choose tenure, amount, and nominee. For DPS, set up auto-debit from your savings account so you never miss a payment.

4

Get your receipt

You'll receive an FDR certificate (physical or digital). Keep it safe — you'll need it for maturity encashment.

📜 Sanchayapatra (National Savings)

সরকারি সঞ্চয়পত্র — সবচেয়ে নিরাপদ ও সবচেয়ে বেশি গ্যারান্টিড রিটার্ন

🏛️ What is Sanchayapatra?

Sanchayapatra are government-backed savings certificates sold through post offices and some bank branches. They offer the highest guaranteed returns in Bangladesh — higher than any bank FDR. Since the government backs them, they are the safest possible investment.

🏆
Best option for most people: 5-Year Bangladesh Sanchayapatra at ~11.83%. If your mother/sister is eligible, Paribar Sanchayapatra at ~11.93% is even better.

📝 How to Buy Sanchayapatra

Requirements: NID, passport-size photo, nominee info, bank account details, and the investment amount (minimum ৳10,000 in multiples of ৳1,000).

1

Go to a National Savings Bureau or designated bank branch

Post offices, Bangladesh Bank branches, and some commercial bank branches sell Sanchayapatra. Sonali Bank, Agrani Bank, and Janata Bank branches are common sellers.

2

Fill the application form

Provide NID, photo, nominee details, and bank account (where profit will be deposited). You'll also need your TIN if you have one.

3

Pay the amount

By bank cheque or pay order (cash may be accepted for smaller amounts). Minimum ৳10,000.

4

Receive your certificate

You'll get a physical certificate. Keep it very safe — this is your proof of investment.

⚠️
Investment ceiling: Maximum ৳50 lakh combining all Sanchayapatra types. 5% source tax is deducted on profit. Early encashment is possible but at lower rates.

📊 Mutual Funds in Bangladesh

মিউচুয়াল ফান্ড কী, কীভাবে কাজ করে, কোথায় বিনিয়োগ করবে — সম্পূর্ণ গাইড

💡 What is a Mutual Fund? (Super Simple Explanation)

Imagine 100 people each put ৳1,000 into a big pot. Total = ৳1,00,000. A professional fund manager takes that pot and buys a mix of stocks, bonds, and other investments. If the pot grows to ৳1,10,000, each person's ৳1,000 is now worth ৳1,100. That's a mutual fund.

🎯
Why it's great for beginners: You don't need to pick individual stocks. A professional does it for you. You can start with as little as ৳1,000/month. Your money is spread across many companies, reducing risk.

🔄 Two Types of Mutual Funds in Bangladesh

📂 Closed-End Funds
Fixed number of units. Listed on DSE. You buy/sell through your brokerage account (like stocks). Fixed maturity (usually 10 years). Many trade at a discount to their actual value (NAV) — which means bargains!
📂 Open-End Funds
No fixed units. NOT on DSE. You buy/sell directly from the fund company at NAV. No maturity date — withdraw anytime. Can invest via SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) = ৳1,000/month. Most flexible option.

📝 How to Invest in Mutual Funds

Option A: Closed-End Funds (via DSE)

1

Same process as buying stocks. You need a brokerage account.

Example: 1JANATAMF trades at ~৳3, 1STPRIMFMF at ~৳19. Very affordable!

3

Just like buying a regular stock. Place a limit order.

Option B: Open-End Funds (directly from fund company)

1

Contact the Asset Management Company

Visit EDGE AMC, Shanta AMC, or LankaBangla AMC website/office.

2

Fill KYC form and provide documents

NID, photo, bank details, nominee info.

3

Set up SIP (monthly auto-investment)

Most allow ৳1,000–5,000/month via auto-debit. This is the best way for beginners.

4

Monitor quarterly NAV reports

Check your fund's NAV weekly on DSE website or the fund company's site.

💡
Tax benefit: Mutual fund investments are eligible for income tax rebate in Bangladesh. You can claim up to 15% tax rebate on your investment amount. This is a significant advantage over bank deposits!

🌾 Agri-Tech Investment Apps

কৃষিতে বিনিয়োগ করো অ্যাপের মাধ্যমে — iFarmer, WeGro, Biniyog

🌱 How These Apps Work

These platforms connect urban investors (like you) with rural farmers who need capital. You invest money → the platform gives it to a farmer for livestock/crops → farmer sells the produce → you get your investment + profit share back.

🔄
The cycle: You invest ৳10,000 in a cow farming project → Farmer raises the cow for 4-6 months → Cow is sold at market → You get back ৳10,000 + 8-15% profit. The farmer also keeps their share.

⚠️ Risks You MUST Know

🐄 Animal death / crop failure▼
Even with insurance, natural disasters, disease outbreaks, or market price drops can affect returns. Insurance may not cover 100% of your investment.
📜 Not BSEC regulated▼
These platforms are NOT regulated by BSEC (the stock market regulator). They operate under different legal frameworks. This means less investor protection compared to bank deposits or Sanchayapatra.
⏰ Locked money▼
Your money is locked for the entire farming cycle (3-8 months). You cannot withdraw early. If you might need the money, this is not for you.
🔍 Transparency concerns▼
Unlike banks, there's limited visibility into exactly how your money is being used. You're trusting the platform's vetting of farmers. Start very small to test.
🔑
Golden Rule: Never invest more than 10-15% of your total savings in agri-tech apps. Start with the minimum amount for your first cycle. Only increase after a successful return.

📝 How to Start

iFarmer or WeGro from Google Play / App Store.

You'll see projects like "Cow Farming Sirajganj — 10% return in 5 months — Min ৳10,000".

4

Invest in a project

Pay via bKash, bank transfer. You'll receive a digital contract.

5

Wait for the cycle to complete

Track updates on the app. At cycle end, profit is distributed to your account.

Historical performance: Gold has returned approximately 9.7% annually in USD and even higher in BDT (because Taka depreciates against USD over time). Gold prices in Bangladesh have increased dramatically over the past decade.

🔄 Ways to Invest in Gold in Bangladesh

🪙 Physical Gold (Jewelry/Coins/Bars)
Buy from BAJUS-registered jewelers. Get hallmarked gold. Store in bank locker (৳3,000–8,000/year). Most traditional method. Making charge: 5-15% on jewelry (wasted on resale).
📱 Digital Gold (Emerging)
Some apps allow fractional gold investment. Still limited in BD compared to India. bKash and some fintech platforms are exploring this space. Check if available when you're ready.

⚠️ Pros and Cons

✅ Pros

  • Universal store of value
  • Beats inflation long-term
  • No counterparty risk (you own it)
  • Culturally valuable in Bangladesh
  • Can be gifted/inherited easily

❌ Cons

  • Making charges lost on jewelry (5-15%)
  • Storage risk — theft, loss
  • No regular income (no dividends/interest)
  • Price can drop short-term
  • Buying 1 bhori requires ~৳2.5 lakh — expensive entry
💡
Beginner tip: With your budget, buying gold jewelry isn't practical (too expensive per bhori). Instead, consider buying small gold coins (1-2 grams) or saving up for gold over time. Some jewelers sell 1-gram gold bars starting at ~৳21,000.

🏘️ Land / Real Estate (জমি-জমা)

জমিতে বিনিয়োগ — বাংলাদেশের সবচেয়ে জনপ্রিয় কিন্তু জটিল বিনিয়োগ

🏘️ The Reality of Land Investment

In Bangladesh, land is culturally considered the most prestigious investment. Prices in Dhaka and surrounding areas have appreciated significantly over decades. However, it comes with serious complexity and risk that most beginners underestimate.

⚠️
Budget reality: With ৳3,000–4,000, you absolutely cannot buy land directly. Land investment typically requires at least ৳3-5 Lakh minimum. This section is for your future knowledge and long-term planning.

⛔ Critical Risks with Land

📜 Title disputes & forgery▼
Bangladesh has a complex land ownership system. Fake documents, multiple claims on the same land, and inheritance disputes are extremely common. ALWAYS hire a lawyer to verify land documents (mutation, khatian, deed). Never buy based on trust alone.
🏗️ Illegal encroachment▼
Someone might occupy your land if you're not physically present. Land grabbing (jomi dakhal) is a real problem, especially in semi-urban and rural areas. You need to visit regularly or have trusted local contacts.
💸 Hidden costs▼
Registration fees (1-3% of deed value), stamp duty, lawyer fees, surveyor fees, annual land tax, and potential development costs. These can add 10-15% to the purchase price.
⏰ Extremely illiquid▼
Selling land in Bangladesh can take months to years. You can't quickly sell land if you need cash urgently. This is the least liquid investment option.

🗺️ Your Action Plan

আজ থেকে শুরু — তোমার সম্পূর্ণ বিনিয়োগ রোডম্যাপ

📅 Month 1: Foundation

  • Open a DPS at your bank (BRAC Bank / DBBL / City Bank) — even ৳1,000/month is fine
  • Set up auto-debit from salary account so you never miss
  • Research mutual funds — visit EDGE AMC or Shanta AMC website
  • Open a bank savings account if you don't have one (needed for everything)
  • This month's investment: DPS auto-debit starts

📅 Month 2-3: Add Mutual Fund

  • Contact an asset management company (EDGE AMC, Shanta, LankaBangla)
  • Start SIP at ৳1,000/month in an open-end fund
  • Or open a BO account and buy closed-end MF units on DSE
  • Keep DPS running on auto-pilot
  • Study AmarStock and DSE — watch market without investing yet

📅 Month 4-6: First Milestone

  • By now your DPS has 4-6 months of deposits building up
  • If you've saved ৳10,000+ separately, buy your first Sanchayapatra
  • Go to nearest Sonali Bank or post office with NID + cheque
  • Consider trying iFarmer with ৳5,000 (1 project, minimum amount)
  • Track all investments in a simple spreadsheet or notebook

📅 Month 6-12: Growing

  • Review your DPS and mutual fund performance
  • Collect first Sanchayapatra quarterly profit (if 3-month type)
  • Evaluate iFarmer cycle completion — did it return as promised?
  • Start learning about stocks for your next level
  • Increase DPS or SIP amount as your income allows
  • Consider your first gold purchase (even 1 gram coin) if budget allows

📅 Year 2+: Diversify & Grow

  • You now have 4-5 investment streams running
  • Add direct stock investment (use your stock market guide!)
  • Increase Sanchayapatra holdings as lump sums become available
  • Start a small gold accumulation plan
  • Begin researching land for 5-10 year future
  • Target: By year 2, have at least 3 active investments
