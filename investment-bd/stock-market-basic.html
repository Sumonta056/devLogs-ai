Stock Market Basics

বাংলাদেশে শেয়ার বাজার কী এবং কীভাবে কাজ করে — একদম শূন্য থেকে শিখুন

💡 What is a Stock / Share?

When you buy a "share" of a company, you become a tiny owner of that company. If the company does well, the value of your share goes up. If it does poorly, the value drops. Think of it like buying a small piece of a shop — if the shop makes profit, you earn too.

🇧🇩
বাংলায়: শেয়ার মানে একটা কোম্পানির ছোট্ট একটা অংশ। তুমি যখন শেয়ার কেনো, তখন তুমি ওই কোম্পানির একজন ক্ষুদ্র মালিক হয়ে যাও।

🏛️ Bangladesh Stock Exchanges

Bangladesh has two stock exchanges:

DSE — Dhaka Stock Exchange
Main exchange. ~600+ listed companies. Located in Nikunja, Dhaka. This is where most trading happens.
CSE — Chittagong Stock Exchange
Smaller exchange. Same companies can be listed on both. Less trading volume.

💰 How You Earn Money

There are two ways to earn from stocks:

📈
Capital Gain: Buy at ৳100, sell at ৳130 → You earned ৳30 profit. This is the most common way. But prices can also fall — that's the risk.
🎁
Dividend: Many companies share their profits with shareholders. This can be cash dividend (you get money in your bank) or stock dividend / bonus share (you get extra shares for free).

🏦 Who Regulates?

BSEC — Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission is the main regulator. They set the rules, protect investors, and oversee DSE & CSE. Think of them as the "traffic police" of the stock market.

CDBL — Central Depository Bangladesh Limited holds your shares electronically in your BO account.

Stock Market Glossary

গুরুত্বপূর্ণ শব্দগুলো জানুন — বাংলা সহ

LTP (Last Traded Price)
সর্বশেষ লেনদেনের দাম
The most recent price at which a share was bought/sold. On AmarStock, this is the main price you see.
YCP (Yesterday's Closing Price)
গতকালের সমাপনী মূল্য
The final price of a stock when the market closed yesterday. Used to calculate today's change.
Change %
পরিবর্তনের হার
How much a stock price has moved today compared to yesterday. Green/positive = up, Red/negative = down.
Volume
লেনদেনের পরিমাণ
Total number of shares traded today. High volume = many people are buying/selling that stock.
High / Low
সর্বোচ্চ / সর্বনিম্ন
The highest and lowest price the stock reached during today's trading session.
Market Cap
বাজার মূলধন
Total value of a company = Share Price × Total Shares. Large cap = big, stable company.
EPS (Earnings Per Share)
শেয়ার প্রতি আয়
Company's profit divided by total shares. Higher EPS = company is earning more per share.
P/E Ratio (Price-to-Earnings)
মূল্য-আয় অনুপাত
Share Price ÷ EPS. Shows how "expensive" a stock is. Lower P/E (under 15) = relatively cheaper.
Dividend Yield
ডিভিডেন্ড ইয়েল্ড
Annual dividend ÷ Share price × 100. Shows the return you get just from dividends. 4%+ is good in BD.
NAV (Net Asset Value)
নিট সম্পদ মূল্য
The actual book value of a company per share. If share price is below NAV, it might be undervalued.
Face Value
অভিহিত মূল্য
The original value assigned to a share (usually ৳10 in BD). Not the market price — just an accounting number.
Circuit Breaker
সার্কিট ব্রেকার
A safety limit. DSE halts trading on a stock if its price moves too much in one day (usually ±10%).
BO Account
বেনিফিশিয়ারি ওনার্স অ্যাকাউন্ট
Your digital account that holds your shares. Like a bank account, but for stocks. Mandatory for trading.

📝 Step-by-Step Process

1

Choose a Brokerage & Go to Their Website

Visit their online BO account opening page. For AmarStock: amarstock.com/open-bo. For CDBL direct: cdbl.com.bd/bo/

2

Enter Mobile Number & Email

You'll receive an OTP for verification. Make sure these are active and accessible.

3

Provide NID Details & Personal Information

Your NID number, name, father's/mother's name, address, date of birth — all from NID.

4

Fill in Nominee & Bank Details

Nominee's NID, your bank account number, branch, and routing number.

5

Upload Documents

Upload photo, NID scan (both sides), nominee's photo and NID, bank cheque leaf/statement.

6

Pay ৳450 (BO Opening Fee)

This is a one-time CDBL charge. Some brokerages may charge additional fees. You can pay via bKash/Nagad/bank transfer.

7

Account Activated (1-3 Business Days)

You'll get your BO account number (16 digits). Now fund your account and start trading!

How to Buy & Sell Stocks

শেয়ার কেনা-বেচার সম্পূর্ণ প্রক্রিয়া

📱 3 Ways to Trade

📱
Mobile/Web App (Best for you): Most brokerage houses have their own app. AmarStock has one too. You log in, search for a stock, place a buy order with price & quantity.
📞
Call Your Broker: Call the brokerage house, tell them "I want to buy 50 shares of SQURPHARMA at ৳215" — they execute the order for you.
🏢
Visit Brokerage Office: Go physically, fill a slip, hand it over. Old-school but some people prefer it.

🛒 Buying Your First Stock (Step by Step)

1

Fund Your BO Account

Transfer money from your bank to your brokerage account. Most accept bank transfer, bKash, or cheque deposit.

2

Research the Stock

Go to amarstock.com/latest-share-price or dsebd.org. Check LTP, P/E ratio, EPS, dividend history.

3

Place a Buy Order

On your trading app: enter the stock code (e.g., SQURPHARMA), quantity (e.g., 15 shares), and price. You can place a "Market Order" (buy at current price) or "Limit Order" (buy only if price drops to your target).

4

Order Gets Matched

If someone is selling at your price, the trade happens. You'll see a confirmation in your app.

5

Settlement (T+2)

Shares appear in your BO account within 2 working days. Money is debited from your brokerage balance.

🔑
Important: The market is open Sun–Thu, 10 AM – 2:30 PM only. You can't buy/sell outside these hours. But you CAN place orders that will execute when the market opens.

💡 Types of Orders

Order TypeHow it WorksBest For
Market OrderBuy/sell at whatever the current price isWhen you want to trade immediately
Limit OrderBuy only at your specified price or lowerWhen you want a better price — more control
💡
As a beginner with ৳3,000-4,000, use Limit Orders. They give you more control. Set your maximum buy price and wait.

🏷️ DSE Company Categories

DSE categorizes all listed companies. As a beginner, you should ONLY invest in A-Category stocks.

CategoryMeaningRiskYour Action

Critical Rule: Z-category stocks might look temptingly cheap (৳2-5 per share) but they are cheap for a reason — these companies are in trouble. Many beginners lose money here. Stay away.

Risks & Golden Rules

শেয়ার বাজারের ঝুঁকি ও সোনালী নিয়ম — এগুলো না জানলে লোকসান হবে

⚠️ Real Risks You Must Know

📉 Market Risk — Prices can drop ▼
Even great companies see their stock prices fall during market crashes. Bangladesh experienced a major crash in 2010-2011 where many lost 50-70% of their investment. The market recovered eventually, but it took years.
🎭 Rumor Risk — "Hot tips" from friends ▼
In Bangladesh's market, rumors spread fast. People will tell you "this stock will double next month." These are almost always wrong or manipulative. NEVER buy based on WhatsApp/Facebook tips. Do your own research.
😱 Emotional Risk — Panic selling ▼
When the market drops 5% in a day, beginners panic and sell at a loss. But if you picked good companies (A-category, strong fundamentals), the price usually recovers. Time in the market beats timing the market.
💸 Liquidity Risk — Can't sell when you want ▼
Some low-volume stocks have very few buyers. You might want to sell but no one is buying. Stick to high-volume stocks (check the Volume column on AmarStock) to avoid this.

🏆 10 Golden Rules for Beginners

1

Only Invest What You Can Afford to Lose

Your ৳3,000-4,000 should be money you won't need for at least 1 year. Never use rent money, loan money, or emergency fund.

2

Stick to A-Category Stocks Only

As a beginner, don't touch Z, B, G, or N categories. A-category means the company pays dividends and holds regular AGMs.

3

Look at P/E Ratio Before Buying

If P/E is above 25-30, the stock might be overpriced. For beginners, look for P/E under 15 in the banking/pharma sector.

4

Prioritize Dividend-Paying Stocks

Dividends give you income even when the price doesn't move. Look for stocks with 4%+ dividend yield and consistent history.

5

Never Buy Based on "Tips" or Rumors

If someone on Facebook says "buy XYZ stock, it'll double!" — run the other way. Do your own research on AmarStock/DSE.

6

Don't Check Prices Every Hour

If you're investing for 1 year, checking every hour causes anxiety. Check once a week. The market has daily ups and downs — that's normal.

7

Start with ONE Stock, Learn, Then Diversify

With ৳3,000-4,000, buy one good stock first. Learn the process. After a few months, you can add more with new savings.

8

Think Long-Term (1 Year Minimum)

Don't try to buy and sell within days. Day trading with small capital will just eat your money in commissions. Hold for at least 1 year.

9

Keep Learning Every Week

Read DSE news, follow market summaries, understand quarterly results. The more you learn, the better your decisions become.

10

Have an Exit Plan

Before buying, decide: "I'll sell if price reaches ৳X (profit target)" and "I'll sell if price drops below ৳Y (stop loss)." Stick to it.

Your Complete Roadmap

তোমার শেয়ার বাজার যাত্রার সম্পূর্ণ রোডম্যাপ — আজ থেকে শুরু

📅 Week 1-2: Preparation

  • Gather documents: NID, passport photo, nominee's NID & photo, bank cheque
  • Open BO account online (AmarStock / BRAC EPL / Mika Securities)
  • Pay ৳450 opening fee
  • While waiting for activation, study AmarStock's price page daily — just observe
  • Learn to read: LTP, Change %, Volume, P/E ratio

📅 Week 3-4: Research & Fund

  • BO account should be active by now
  • Transfer ৳3,000-4,000 to your brokerage account
  • Research 3-5 A-category stocks on AmarStock and DSE
  • Check their P/E, EPS, dividend history, and recent news
  • Shortlist 1-2 stocks you understand and feel confident about
  • Use the Calculator tab above to check how many shares you can buy

📅 Week 5: First Purchase!

  • Place a Limit Order for your chosen stock
  • Start with your strongest pick (e.g., SQURPHARMA)
  • Don't use all your money — keep ৳500-1,000 as buffer
  • Once order is executed, celebrate your first investment! 🎉
  • Screenshot your portfolio for the memory

📅 Month 2-6: Hold & Learn

  • Check your portfolio once a week, not every day
  • Follow quarterly earnings reports of your company
  • Read DSE market summaries
  • Start saving for your next investment (even ৳500/month adds up)
  • Learn about new sectors — IT, textiles, energy
  • Don't panic if price drops 5-10% — that's normal

📅 Month 6-12: Grow

  • Collect your first dividend! (If the company declares one)
  • Re-evaluate your stock: Has anything fundamentally changed?
  • Consider adding a second stock with new savings for diversification
  • Start understanding technical analysis basics (support/resistance levels)
  • At the 1-year mark, decide: hold, sell for profit, or add more
