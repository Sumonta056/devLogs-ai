CCA-F Road Map · Mocks · 32
TWO HABITS TO AVOID
Two things you must avoid in mocks
There is a huge difference between the mocks and the real exam.
AVOID
Reading the options first
Read only the question. Work out the concept and the core idea before you look at any option.
AVOID
Leaving a confusing question behind
After the mock, learn which option fits which case for that question.

CCA-F Road Map · Mocks · 34
DON’T MEMORISE MOCK ANSWERS
Answer the question before you look at the options
01
Read the scenario
02
Identify the real problem
03
Decide what the architecture should do
04
Think of the best solution
05
Now look at the options
06
Eliminate the wrong ones
07
Choose the best remaining answer
Why: Mock options can mislead. If you only recognise a familiar option, you’ll struggle when the scenario changes.

CCA-F Road Map · Tips · 40
LEAVE NO GAPS
Cover everything, then pick the best answer
Prepare all six scenarios
Each carries 15 questions and you don’t know which four you’ll get. Don’t become strong in only one. Don’t gamble.
Skip no topic in the five domains
“I already know this” and “it’s a small section” cost marks. Know what it is, why it exists, when to use it and what happens when it fails.
Rank the valid solutions
Several answers can technically work. The question asks for the preferred one for this scenario.

BUILD MOMENTUM
Take the sure points first, then come back
Skip the first 10 if needed
The first few can be hard on purpose. Avoid the nervous trap.

Answer what you know immediately.
Manage time aggressively

Use the first pass wisely

Accept you won’t solve all
Don’t let one question eat 10–20 minutes.
Master the art of ignore
Don’t know it? Flag it. Next question.
CCA-F Road Map · Exam day · 47
READ THE WORDING
One word can change what the question asks
It sounds obvious, but it matters. Watch for these:

should
best
least
most appropriate
first
after
before
without
only
